# Essay Introduction Exercise

Write a 300 word (ish) introduction, explaining the meaning of the project title & the key terms in it. This must contain at least two in-text citations. You should use the Harvard citation style to reference the sources and evidence used.

You can put your references at the end of the document (these do  not contribute to word count).
But I do need an "Evaluation Table of Resources" with the cited sources in, so you can show selection and evaluation.

[A Quick Guide to Harvard Referencing | Citation Examples](https://www.scribbr.co.uk/referencing/harvard-style/)
