## Essay Planning

Make an essay plan

1. To get between your introduction and answer what are you going to need to show / explain ?
2. Use each sentence in your Conclusion as thing you need to talk about in the main body.
3. What info / source helps do that, are you missing any sources?
4. Are there other opinions and why do you think yours is better?
5. Submit these in “CyberEPQ Full Project”
